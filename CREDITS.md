# Credits — Space Samurai

Assets are linked and downloaded from creator source pages via the
[Free Asset Index](https://canop3n3r.github.io/free-asset-index/). Licenses are
CC0 or CC-BY only. Placeholders fill gaps when a format is unavailable.

## 3D kits used

- "Space Kit" by Kenney (https://opengameart.org/content/space-kit / https://kenney.nl/assets/space-kit), licensed under CC0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
  Used: spacecraft (`spaceCraft1`–`3`), rocks and meteors, modular buildings
  (`buildingOpen`, `buildingCorner`, corridors, `frameHigh`, `metalStructure`),
  `stationLarge`, `satelliteDish`, `portal`, astronaut, alien, robot, barrels,
  consoles, `laserSabel` / `laserSabelOff`, `itemWeapon`, and the net / character textures.
  Files under `public/assets/free/opengameart/space-kit/`.
  Loaded as OBJ + MTL so kit colors and textures stay intact. The player silhouette
  is a composed neon samurai with the Kenney laser saber as the glow blade.

## Blade note

Glow blade uses Kenney Space Kit `laserSabel` (CC0) with neon emissive tint. Optional future swap:

- "Low-poly Sci-Fi Swords" by Glitch Squirrel (https://opengameart.org/content/low-poly-sci-fi-swords), licensed under CC-BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
  Unitypackage-only at source for now.

- "Cyberpunk Game Kit" by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com/packs/cyberpunkgamekit.html), licensed under CC0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
  Drive-folder distribution; neon street placeholders used in v1.

- "Ultimate Spaceships Pack" by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatespaceships.html), licensed under CC0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
  Kenney Space Kit craft used instead for v1.
